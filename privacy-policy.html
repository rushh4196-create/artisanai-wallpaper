Privacy Policy — ARTISANAI Daily Pins (Pinterest app)

Last updated: 26 November 2026

What this app is

ARTISANAI Daily Pins is a personal tool built for a single user — the owner of the ARTISANAI Pinterest account. It creates one Pin per day of original AI-generated artwork on the owner's own Pinterest board, using the Pinterest API. It is not offered to other users.

What data the app accesses

Through the Pinterest API, and only with the account owner's OAuth authorisation, the app accesses:

  • The owner's own boards (to find the board a Pin belongs to)
  • The owner's own Pins (to confirm a Pin was created)
  • The owner's basic account information (to confirm which account is connected)

The app does not access any other Pinterest user's boards, Pins, or personal information. It never asks for or stores Pinterest usernames, passwords or session cookies — authentication is only through Pinterest OAuth 2.0.

What data the app stores

The app does not store Pinterest data. Images pinned are original artwork generated for the owner, and a Pin is created directly through the Pinterest API. No Pinterest data is copied to any other server, sold, or shared with any third party.

How data is used

Pinterest data is used only for the single purpose described above: publishing the owner's own artwork to the owner's own board. It is not used for advertising, analytics, profiling, or training any AI or machine-learning model.

Data deletion

If the owner disconnects the app or asks for deletion, all stored credentials are removed. Any Pinterest data held at that time is deleted within 30 days, as required by Pinterest's Developer Terms.
