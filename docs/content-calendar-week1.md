## Rules (from the plan)
- Portrait is **composed, not cropped** — separate prompt, vertical composition.
- No copyrighted characters, logos, or celebrity likenesses.
- One live wallpaper per day; motion is **minor** — alive in 2 seconds, then settles.
- Keep a **7-day buffer** pre-generated so a failed morning never leaves the app empty.
- Human spot-check once a week (15 min) — review the week's drops + next 7 buffer days.
- Every live wallpaper ships its static master as a fallback.
